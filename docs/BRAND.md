# Aroh brand note

## Name

**Aroh** is derived from Sanskrit **āroha** (`आरोह`): ascent, rising or the act
of mounting. It gives the project an active, forward-moving name without making
the consumer brand feel ceremonial.

Preferred product construction:

- Aroh
- Aroh OS
- Aroh One — reserved as a possible prototype-device name

Working line:

> Less screen. More agency.

## Creative numerology

Using the Chaldean naming system:

```text
A(1) + R(2) + O(7) + H(5) = 15 → 6
```

Numerology is used only as a creative naming filter, not as evidence of product
or commercial outcomes.

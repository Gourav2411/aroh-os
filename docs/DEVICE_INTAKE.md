## Safe information to send

Photos of the phone, model number, About Phone screen, chipset/RAM/storage,
Android build, bootloader state and community ROM links are useful.

Before sharing screenshots, cover:

- IMEI and serial numbers;
- phone numbers and email addresses;
- Wi-Fi names and IP addresses;
- account identifiers; and
- unlock codes or recovery keys.
